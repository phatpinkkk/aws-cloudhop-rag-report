During my internship with **AWS First Cloud AI Journey**, I attended three community events. Each event was quite different, which made them useful in different ways. One focused mainly on sharing technical and career experiences, another turned AWS architecture knowledge into a team-based challenge, and the last showed how teams had turned AI and cloud ideas into working products during a hackathon.

What I liked most about these events was that they gave me perspectives outside my own CloudHop RAG project. I could see how other people approached AWS, how cloud services are used in different types of systems, and how technical ideas become more meaningful when they are connected to real problems.

### [Event 1 - FCAJ Community Day](4.1-Event1/)

**Date:** June 6, 2026  
**Role:** Attendee

A community sharing event covering AWS, GraphRAG, teamwork, Docker, and career development. The session that stayed with me most was the story of moving from IT Helpdesk to Senior Sysadmin and gradually toward Cloud and DevOps.

### [Event 2 - Cloud Architect](4.2-Event2/)

**Date:** June 20, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee

A gamified AWS architecture event where teams answered scenario-based questions about cloud services and system design. It helped me realize how broad cloud computing really is and how many different areas have to work together in a complete architecture.

### [Event 3 - AI Agent Build Week](4.3-Event3/)

**Date:** July 25, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee

A sharing event where hackathon teams presented how they designed and built AI-agent applications with AWS. I was especially impressed by the variety of ideas and the creativity of the solutions, from conversational ordering and live operational dashboards to financial-risk analysis and AI-assisted solution architecture.
