My internship experience with **AWS First Cloud AI Journey** gave me the chance to move gradually from learning core AWS services to working on a complete AI project with my team. During the first few weeks, I focused on building a practical understanding of AWS, including EC2, S3, IAM, networking, and the basic tools used to manage cloud resources.

As the internship progressed, my team and I developed **AWS CloudHop RAG**, a multi-hop Retrieval-Augmented Generation system for question answering on HotpotQA. My main contribution was on the retrieval and evaluation side of the project. I worked on preparing and validating the benchmark data, building and improving the retrieval pipeline, testing hybrid and multi-hop retrieval methods, and analyzing retrieval quality, answer accuracy, and latency.

Working on the project also gave me a better understanding of how different parts of a real system come together. While my teammates worked on different parts of the AWS deployment and application, I contributed to validating the retrieval pipeline and checking whether the final system was returning the right evidence and answers. This was especially useful because I could see how the retrieval experiments I worked on connected with the deployed backend, AWS services, and the final application.

This worklog summarizes the main work I completed with my team over 8 weeks, from **08/06/2026 to 31/07/2026**.

**Week 2:** [Amazon S3, IAM, and AWS networking](1.2-week2/)

**Week 4:** [HotpotQA preparation and retrieval baselines](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Advanced hybrid and multi-hop retrieval](1.5-week5/)
